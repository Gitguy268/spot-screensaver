# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-18)

First release.

- A loop of nap → dream → chase (inside his dream) → wake, with random lengths, prey and camera shots.
- 3D style (three.js): Spot is an AI-generated 3D model of him in his blanket, brought to life without a skeleton (head nod, breathing, eyelids, ear, paw and lip twitches, tail thumps); the dream chase uses the galloping dog from Spot-light HD in a golden garden with depth of field.
- Vector style (SVG): Spot traced from his portrait in layered shapes, the same motions, a vector cabin and a scrolling dream meadow with a traced gallop.
- "Both": the styles take turns every loop.
- macOS screen saver bundle (arm64 + x86_64, ad-hoc signed) with an Options sheet for style, quality and sound.
- Browser version with controls, keyboard shortcuts and URL options; optional synthesised sound.
